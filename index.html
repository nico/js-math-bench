Function Max ULP?ULP = Unit in the Last Place, the gap between two adjacent representable doubles near the exact result. A ULP error of 0 means the result is exact; 0.5 means halfway to the next double (correctly rounded); 1.0 means off by one representable value (faithfully rounded); >1 means inaccurate. Hover a cell to see the worst-case input, expected value, and actual value. Mean ULP Correctly Rounded?Percentage of test inputs where the result is within 0.5 ULP of the exact value, meaning the implementation returned the closest representable double. Faithfully Rounded?Result is within 1.0 ULP of the exact value. The result is one of the two doubles adjacent to the exact value. Accuracy?Accuracy score = 100 / (1 + mean ULP). Perfect accuracy = 100. Every ULP of error counts: mean ULP 0.1 = 90.9, mean ULP 0.5 = 66.7, mean ULP 1.0 = 50. Large errors devastate the score. Ops/sec Perf Bonus?Performance bonus = 20 × (ops/sec) / 1000M, capped at 20. Rewards throughput but capped so speed can't compensate for inaccuracy. Total?Total = Accuracy + Perf Bonus (max 120). Accuracy dominates (0-100) while performance is a secondary bonus (0-20). Overall score is the geometric mean across all functions.
